The seats

Government

The Council and the City Police. Laws, taxes, arrests, a prison of 40, and a police chief with a private bank account.

Criminals

The Families and the Saltdogs. Turf, income, bribes, and whether the two gangs keep the peace.

World

The Wire (the city's internet), weather, fires, ships and strikes. It frames the news; the dice are code.

The rotation

Every model holds every seat once every 3 weeks. Same city, same rules, same seed; only the seating changes.
