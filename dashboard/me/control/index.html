Schedule and spending

Schedule

When on, the first live run plays a day every 10 minutes. You can close this page.

Spending

Runs

  • Step plays one day: it starts within a minute, even with the schedule off, and you can close the page.
  • Pause and Resume stop and restart the schedule for that run. Nothing is lost: it carries on from the next day.
  • Check answers "can the next day play?": it looks for a missing key, an unpinned model or the spending cap, without spending anything.
  • Ledger opens the ledger page on this run: every model call, its tokens, cost and who paid.
  • End finishes the run for good. It moves to the archive on the home page, its secrets open (a study run's once its whole triple has ended), and its data is saved to the repo within the hour.
  • A run that can't play pauses itself and says why in the log.
